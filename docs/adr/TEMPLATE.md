# NNNN. Short title in imperative or descriptive form

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-slug.md)

## Context

What is the situation that motivates this decision? What forces are at play — technical, political, social, project-scoped? State the facts that a reader who has not been in the room would need in order to evaluate the decision.

## Decision

The change or choice we are making. Phrase it in the active voice: "We will …", not "We should …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or different as a result. Include both the intended positives and the accepted trade-offs. New follow-up work goes here too.
